# Digital Combat Grid V1.0

Owner approved Concept B visual direction in conversation on 2026-11-27. Concept A realistic battlefield rejected as primary direction. Approval is not publication authorization. Sector Rebound retains its existing runtime art, launcher and game identity.

## Reusable art rules
- Grounded stylized low-poly 3D, elevated semi-isometric viewpoint, simple silhouettes and consistent geometry.
- Matte graphite and metallic gray, modular grid floors, industrial maze walls; amber primary accent, cyan secondary accent. Controlled glow, clean impact flashes, no excessive particles or weathering.
- One readable vehicle silhouette, simple turret and two coherent tracks, modest detail. Avoid photorealistic military scenes, extra barrels, impossible perspective, clutter and invented insignia.
- Keep title and creator credit readable; for future derivatives prefer separate typeset layers. No version embedded in evergreen artwork. Use the existing approved image unchanged as reference; related Operation Phosphor aesthetic is a direction, not an established shared canon.
- Reusable generation brief: stylized low-poly digital combat arena, grounded angular armored vehicle, matte graphite modular walls and floor grid, restrained amber and cyan accents, clean ricochet trail, coherent elevated perspective, minimal texture, no lettering for background/vehicle assets. Inspect geometry, track count, projectile continuity, grid alignment and text before approval.
