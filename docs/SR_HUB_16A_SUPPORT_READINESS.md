# SR-HUB-16A — Beta support readiness

Proposed lightweight workflow for a solo developer; not an active support service or response-time promise. Use existing approved publisher identity and monitored contact once confirmed. Keep private correspondence and receipts out of public repositories.

## Player-facing support guidance (draft)
For a problem, include game version, Android version, device model, game mode, what happened and steps to reproduce. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information. Do not post backup text, private IP addresses, passwords, signing material or other private data in public comments. If progress seems missing, do not uninstall or clear data before checking backups and the installed app identity.

## Internal triage
P0: suspected data loss, security/privacy exposure, unsafe update — stop distribution of affected candidate, preserve evidence, investigate before resuming.
P1: startup crash, blocking controls, failed install/update or critical multiplayer failure — reproduce on exact version and prioritize correction.
P2: visual defects, nonblocking usability, balance — triage after reproducible evidence.
P3: suggestions, new features — roadmap review, no promise of delivery.

Record version, scope, reproducibility, severity, sanitized evidence, disposition and regression test. Keep reporter identity private. Use no new analytics, crash upload, form backend or account system without owner approval and review.

## Open gates
Confirm public publisher/contact, monitored channel, audience, scope, host and policy link; review exact signed build, legal/platform requirements, artwork rights, physical QA and durable archive. No publishing or deployment authorized.
